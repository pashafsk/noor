📄 Privacy Policy for Noor
Effective Date: January 10, 2026

1. Introduction
Noor ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Noor mobile application.

2. Information Collection and Use
Noor does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

Location Data: The App requires access to your device's location (GPS) to calculate the precise direction of the Qibla based on your current coordinates.

Processing: Location data is processed locally on your device and is "ephemeral" (it is used for the calculation and then immediately discarded).

Storage: We do not store your location on our servers or share it with any third parties.

Audio Streaming: The App streams Quranic recitations from third-party servers.

Data Involved: When streaming, standard technical data (such as your IP address) is sent to the audio hosting provider to fulfill the request. No personal information is attached to these requests.

3. Permissions
To provide its core features, Noor requests the following permissions:

Precise Location (GPS): Used solely for Qibla direction calculation.

Internet Access: Used to stream audio recitations.

Network State: Used to check for a connection before attempting to stream audio.

4. Third-Party Services
Noor may use open-source libraries (such as the Adhan library) to perform astronomical calculations. These libraries operate entirely offline and do not collect data.

5. Children’s Privacy
Noor is a religious and educational tool intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

6. Security
We value your trust. Since we do not collect personal data, there is no risk of your personal information being leaked from our systems. All audio streaming is conducted over secure HTTPS connections.

7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date."
